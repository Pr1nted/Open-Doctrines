## 13. How to accept

**Submitting a Contribution is acceptance of this agreement**, in the
version current at the time you submit. That is stated in

Do both of these on your first pull request:

1. Add yourself to [CLA-SIGNATURES.md](CLA-SIGNATURES.md), in the same pull

2. Sign off each commit:

## Why this exists

Stated plainly, because a document that takes rights should say why it
takes them.

**The project needs to be relicensable as one work.** Clause 5 of LICENSE
lets the Licensor release the Software under different terms later,
including commercial ones — that is the funding path for a game that is
otherwise free. That power reaches only rights the Licensor holds. A
non-exclusive inbound licence, which is what the previous version of clause
4 of LICENSE asked for, leaves each contributor still owning their patch,
still able to license it elsewhere, and still holding rights that constrain
what the project as a whole can become. Assignment removes that constraint
in one step instead of leaving it to be discovered years later, when the
patch is load-bearing and its author is unreachable.

**What it does not, and cannot, do.** No agreement can make someone stop
being the author of what they wrote, as a matter of fact or of history; the
commit log will always say who wrote it, and clause 6 says so explicitly.
What clause 2 transfers is the economic right — the right to copy, change,
sell and license. Clause 4 reaches for the rest, and reaches as far as the
law allows, which in some countries is not all the way. Nothing here
pretends to be more airtight than it is.

**Clause 6 is the price of clause 2, and it is worth paying.** An
assignment CLA with no licence back means a contributor cannot reuse their
own function in their own project. That is a real cost to them and no gain
to the project, and it is the reason such CLAs put people off. Clause 6
removes it: you give up exclusivity, not your own work.

**As of this version, the Licensor is the sole author of the Software** —
every commit in the repository is theirs. This agreement is here before the
first outside contribution rather than after, which is the only time it can
be introduced without asking anyone to re-sign for work they already gave.
