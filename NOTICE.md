## Data deliberately not used

Recorded because the absence is a decision, not an oversight, and
because the next person to look for a world ethnic dataset will find
the same one and wonder why it is not here.

### GREG (Geo-referencing of Ethnic Groups), ETH Zurich — removed 2026-07

- **Was used for:** Ethnic group polygons behind minorities.json
- **Why it went:** No licence is offered. The download page asks only that the dataset be cited "when using the GREG data for your research" — a citation request under research framing, not a grant, and silent on redistribution or on shipping derived data in a game. GREG is itself a digitisation of the 1964 Soviet Atlas Narodov Mira, so ETH is in no position to sublicense the underlying work either; asking them could not have produced a usable answer.
- **Replaced by:** tools/data/ethnic_groups.json — project-authored country compositions from public-domain reference material, plus hand-drawn concentration boxes. See tools/generate_minorities.py.

### ACOR (oil and gas field polygons), ETH Zurich — removed 2026-07

- **Was used for:** Oil field centroids behind the oil layer of resources.json
- **Why it went:** Same shape of problem as GREG: no licence text, a citation requirement only, and the polygons are digitised from a 1982 atlas.
- **Replaced by:** tools/data/oil_fields.json — a project-authored list of named producing regions with play-balance weights. See load_oil_fields() in tools/overlay_real_data.py.
