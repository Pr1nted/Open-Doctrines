# Getting listed on AlternativeTo

## Why this one is worth the twenty minutes

People type "free alternative to Hearts of Iron IV" as a literal search, and
AlternativeTo ranks for exactly that shape of query. It is an accumulation
surface, not an announcement: a listing keeps working for years with no further
input, which matches how this game actually grows — 96% of its traffic today
comes from people browsing or searching, and about 1% from anyone being told.

And the slot is empty. Checked on 2026-09-11, the
[Hearts of Iron page](https://alternativeto.net/software/hearts-of-iron/)
carried five alternatives:

| | licence | platforms |
|---|---|---|
| Crusader Kings | paid | Mac, Windows, Steam, GOG, Xbox, PlayStation |
| Europa Universalis | paid | Mac, Windows, Linux, Steam |
| Command: Modern Operations | paid | Windows |
| RealPolitiks | paid | Mac, Windows, Linux, Android |
| World Conqueror 4 | paid | Windows, Android, iPhone |

**Not one of them is free. Not one is open source. Not one runs in a browser.**
AlternativeTo's sidebar filters on precisely those three properties, so this
game would be the only result for a visitor who ticks any of them — on a page
someone reached by searching for a cheaper way to play Hearts of Iron.

It would also list more platforms than any entry there.

## Submitting

Free, self-serve, no editorial queue. The listing is invisible until approved
and editable by anyone afterwards.

1. Create an account at <https://alternativeto.net>. **Use an address on a
   domain you control** if you have one.
2. **Verify the email before anything else.** Submission is blocked until you
   do, and the form gives no useful hint about why.
3. User icon, top right → **Suggest new application**. Fill the fields below.

There is no direct deep link to the form here on purpose: AlternativeTo answers
automated requests with 403, so any URL in this file would be a guess, and a
guess that 404s wastes more time than a menu path costs.

**Then do the second step, which is the one that matters.** Creating the listing
gets a page nobody visits. Being ATTACHED to the pages people already land on is
the whole point:

> On each target page below: **Contribute to this page** → **Suggest
> Alternatives** → search for OpenDoctrines and add it.

Submitting your own project is allowed and normal; AlternativeTo has a "claim
your listing" flow for makers. What the community does punish is astroturfing —
so add it once, honestly, and do not vote on it from other accounts.

## The fields

**Name:** `OpenDoctrines`

**Platforms:** Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, Web / Browser

**Licence:** Free · Open Source
*(the source is public; the licence is non-commercial, so tick Free and Open
Source and let the description carry the nuance rather than overstating it)*

**Short description**
> A free, open-source grand strategy game that runs in a browser. Run a
> country's industry, armies, research and politics across 1641 provinces and
> six historical scenarios, from 1914 to the modern day.

**Full description**
> OpenDoctrines is a grand strategy game about the whole of a state: its
> industry, its armies, its research, its politics and its neighbours.
>
> Provinces have population, industry, fortification, resources and an ethnic
> composition. Countries have a treasury, a research programme, a political
> compass, claims on their neighbours and opinions about each other. Rebellions
> spawn from unrest and hold real territory, wars pull in guarantee chains, and
> ceasefires are negotiated over provinces, money and dropped claims.
>
> Six scenarios on a 1641-province world map: Modern Day (185 countries, with
> populations from World Bank totals), 1914, 1918, 1939, 1945 and the 1962
> missile crisis. There is a map editor with a procedural generator, multiplayer
> that needs no port forwarding, and a WebAssembly mod SDK with bindings for
> thirteen languages. The interface is translated into 21 languages.
>
> It plays in a browser with no download and no account, inside a Discord voice
> channel as an Activity, or as a desktop and Android download. It is an alpha:
> playable, and not finished — there is no tutorial yet.

## Pages to list it as an alternative to

In rough order of how well the intent matches. The first two are the ones that
matter; the rest are cheap once the listing exists.

- [Hearts of Iron](https://alternativeto.net/software/hearts-of-iron/) — the empty slot above
- [Europa Universalis](https://alternativeto.net/software/europa-universalis/)
- [RealPolitiks](https://alternativeto.net/software/realpolitiks) — nearest in scope, also paid
- Crusader Kings, World Conqueror 4, Victoria, Supremacy 1914, Conflict of Nations

Supremacy 1914 and Conflict of Nations are worth doing specifically because they
are browser-based, which is the one property this game shares with them and with
nothing else on the Hearts of Iron page.

## After it is live

Nothing, and that is the point. Unlike a post it does not decay, and unlike a
storefront it needs no upkeep beyond editing the description when the game stops
being an alpha. Similar surfaces worth the same twenty minutes each:
LibreGameWiki, OpenSourceGameClones, and Wikipedia's *List of open-source video
games*.
