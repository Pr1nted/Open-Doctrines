# The first tournament — paste-ready

The follow-up to the 1.3.2b launch (`docs/growth/launch-1.3.2b.md`): one shared
Modern Day world, up to 16 players, one turn a day. Seats are limited and the
join code is public — first come. Nothing here is posted by a workflow: itch's
devlog is dashboard-only, and the in-game board needs the developer badge.

Key facts, in one place, so the three posts stay consistent:

- **Map:** Modern Day. Everyone plays a real country, themselves, start to finish.
- **Seats:** 16, first come. Claim one now and the country is held for you —
  even across a server restart. Just rejoin with the same account.
- **Pace:** one turn every 24 hours. A full day to give orders; miss a deadline
  and *If I miss the deadline, do nothing* keeps a stand-in off your turn.
- **Join code:** `M2PR-DZG5` — Multiplayer → Join, code only, address blank.
  Web players join the same way.
- **Client:** 1.3.2b or newer. Browser build at <https://opendoctrines.pages.dev/play>
  or the desktop download on itch. **Older builds cannot connect to a hosted
  game** — that is the bug 1.3.2b fixed.
- **Lobby opens:** now (claim a seat). **First turn opens:** Wednesday 21 October.
- **Caution:** experimental. First hosted tournament, free server, days-old
  long-campaign code. Rough edges expected; report them.

## 1. itch.io devlog

**Where:** itch dashboard → **Devlog → New post**.

**Title:** The first tournament — 16 seats, one turn a day (and it's an experiment)

**Body:**

Hello strategists!

1.3.2b made multiplayer the real thing — so now we're going to stress it in
public. The first OpenDoctrines tournament: one shared Modern Day world, up to
16 players, one turn a day. A slow campaign you check in on when you like, not an
evening you have to clear.

### How it works

- The map is **Modern Day**. Everyone starts as a real country and plays it
  themselves — research, economy, diplomacy, the lot.
- **One turn resolves every 24 hours.** You have a full day to give your orders;
  miss a deadline and you can set *If I miss the deadline, do nothing* so nobody
  plays your turn for you.
- **16 seats, first come.** Claim one now and your country is held for you, even
  across a server restart — just rejoin with the same account.

### How to join

1. **Update to 1.3.2b** — the browser build at opendoctrines.pages.dev/play, or
   the desktop download here on itch. Older builds cannot connect to a hosted
   game; this is the release that fixed that.
2. **Multiplayer → Join**, enter the code **M2PR-DZG5**, and leave the address
   blank. Web players join the same way.
3. **Pick your country.** The lobby is open now; the first turn opens
   **Wednesday, 21 October**.

### A word of caution — this one is an experiment

This is the first time we've run a hosted tournament: a free server, and a
long-campaign codebase that is only days old. Expect rough edges. The server can
restart between turns — your seat and your country are held, so rejoining is all
it takes — turns may hiccup, and we'll be watching the whole way. If something
breaks, tell us; that's half the point of running it in the open. Call it
Season 0: the shakedown cruise, not the grand final.

See you on the map.
