Mods for OpenDoctrines

Every mod here is hosted by the person who made it. This page is a directory: it tells you what exists and sends you to them.

Publish a mod Write one

Newest first. No account needed to browse or download.

Nobody here has run these mods. They are not reviewed, not tested and not approved. Where a security check is shown it is a lookup of the hash the author declared — a clean result means nobody has reported those bytes, not that the mod is safe. The game will never install a mod by itself; you add it yourself, and that is on purpose.
